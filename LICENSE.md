overlap, and reordered by usefulness. Many meanings, examples and origins were rewritten or
corrected, and some entries were removed or added. The stories (`stories.csv`), the decode words
(`decode.csv`) and the entries marked `curated` were written for this deck.

Word frequencies used for selection come from [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq)
(used only by `tools/import_wikipedia.py`, not distributed).
